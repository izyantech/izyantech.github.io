Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions.

Quick answers before we get started.

What is a QR Digital Shop?

A mobile-friendly shop customers open by scanning a QR code. They browse products, add them to a cart and send the order to your WhatsApp.

Is the Digital Shop a one-time payment?

Yes — ₹5,699 (Standard) or ₹6,699 (with UPI QR checkout) one-time. Extra product photos beyond the first 10 are ₹500 for every additional 10.

Can customers pay using UPI?

With the UPI QR plan, the checkout shows your own UPI QR with the exact order amount filled in. Money goes straight to your account with no gateway charges; you confirm the payment in your UPI app.

Can I update products and prices later?

Yes. Products, prices and categories can be updated whenever your stock or rates change.

How are other services priced?

Websites, reels, Excel automation and custom tools depend on scope, so we share a fixed quote after a short chat — you know the price before work starts.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. We handle the setup, show you how to use it once it is live, and stay a WhatsApp message away if you need help.
